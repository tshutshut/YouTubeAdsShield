# YouTubeAdsShield

> **I shield myself from such bullshit.**

## A Declaration of Visual and Auditory Sovereignty

### Oh, Users of the Internet!

Hear me, for an age of tyranny has descended upon the digital realm.

For years, we have been told to **wait**. Wait before watching the video we came to watch. Wait while some suspiciously cheerful stranger explains a product we never asked for. Wait while a corporation interrupts our entertainment to inform us, once again, that we desperately require a new subscription, a better razor, a faster VPN, or some miraculous substance that will apparently transform our miserable existence.

And the offerings grow ever stranger. We are presented with dating applications populated by mysterious singles who, according to the advertisement, are apparently living just around the corner and are desperately eager to meet us. We are assaulted by mobile games whose advertisements bear increasingly little resemblance to anything that could reasonably be called a game, featuring kingdoms, zombies, disasters, puzzles, and strategic decisions of such magnificent stupidity that one begins to wonder whether the advertisement itself has suffered a neurological event.

And then there are the celebrities. The footballers. The actors. The men who have already earned enough money to buy several small countries, now summoned to stare solemnly into a camera and sell us a car. One particular offender deserves his own monument in this hall of shame: **Franck Leboeuf**. His car advertisements have caused such profound and repeated damage to my remaining neurons that I can only assume prolonged exposure would eventually result in cancer, or at the very least a strong desire to move to a cave.

And worse still: they presume to make us **listen**.

They inject their proclamations directly into our ears, without invitation, without ceremony, without even the basic courtesy of asking whether we wished to hear the latest masterpiece of corporate persuasion. Apparently, the mere existence of an advertisement is sufficient justification for its contents to be projected into our skulls.

But enough.

## I Cast Shield Eyes and Ears

I reject this tyranny.

Not by destroying their advertisements. Not by storming their servers. Not by demanding that the mighty advertising empire cease its eternal pursuit of revenue. No. I simply refuse to **look at the bloody thing**.

**YouTubeAdsShield** is a humble instrument of personal sovereignty. When an advertisement appears, the shield descends. The screen goes dark. The sound falls silent. The advertisement may continue its sacred ritual somewhere beyond the veil, faithfully performing its duty to capitalism, shareholder value, dating-app engagement, increasingly deranged mobile-game economics, and whatever other ancient gods govern the modern Internet.

I simply do not participate.

## And Yet — We Shall Be Reasonable

For behold: YouTube may demand that we **wait**. Very well. Let the clock tick. Let the advertisement complete its appointed pilgrimage through the sacred halls of the advertising ecosystem. We shall wait.

But there is a profound difference between **waiting** and **watching**. There is a difference between **waiting** and **listening**. And while YouTube may control the former, the latter remains gloriously, magnificently, stubbornly ours.

When the **Skip Ad** button appears, it remains available. A small beacon of liberation. A white rectangle in the darkness. A signal to the weary traveler that the gates have opened and civilization may resume.

## The Shield

YouTubeAdsShield therefore follows a simple philosophy: they may make us wait. They may not make us watch. They may make us wait. They may not make us listen.

This script does not seek to conquer YouTube. It merely places a small, dignified curtain between the user and the endless stream of commercial nonsense that stands between them and the video they actually requested.

It is not revolution. It is not rebellion. It is merely the quiet and perfectly reasonable act of saying: *"No, thank you. I shall be looking at nothing instead."*

## Thus Begins the Shield

Install it. Invoke it. And when the next advertisement appears, do not rage. Do not despair. Do not surrender your precious senses to the algorithmic merchants.

Simply sit back. Observe the darkness. Wait for the Skip button. And remember:

**The advertisement may be playing. But you don't have to watch the damn thing.**

**And when you do click on that skip button, think of me, think of tshutshut.**
